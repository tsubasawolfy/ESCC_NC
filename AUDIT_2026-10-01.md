### GO scope and provenance

Two historical GO archive families were found: BP/MF/CC row counts 1,194/237/121 and 1,290/272/137. Both are compatible with overlap subsets of the historical 204 genes, but the exact historical submitted-list metadata and dates cannot be independently verified. Neither family has an FDR-significant methylation text match. EHMT2 membership in the historical archive cannot be transferred to the corrected query because EHMT2 is absent from the new selected list. Enrichr's library-default universe is used; no tested-gene custom universe is supplied or claimed. After explicit user authorization, a fresh 179-symbol query was submitted to the [official Enrichr API](https://maayanlab.cloud/Enrichr/help#api) on 2026-10-01 (11:49 UTC; list ID 138954033). The readback retained all 179 symbols, with C1orf226 and CXorf49B uppercased by the service. Raw JSON, canonical TSV exports, the exact original list and fingerprints were archived. Script 02 successfully processed these fresh results.

The corrected query returned **three methylation text matches**, with adjusted p values .374084, .400280 and .576112: none meet FDR < .05. They involve MEN1 or ING2; there are **zero EHMT2 overlap memberships**, because EHMT2 is not in the corrected query. Nine other GO terms pass the service's per-library adjusted-p threshold (one BP and eight MF); this does not support a methylation-enrichment claim. These are exploratory library-default-universe results, not a custom-background confirmatory analysis. The R `enrichR` live branch was not executed in this environment; the actual service API and the script's archive-processing path were exercised.
